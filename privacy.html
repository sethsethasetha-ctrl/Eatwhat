Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Local policy draft. The developer's public contact details and the hosting arrangements for this website must be confirmed before this page is used as a public App Store privacy policy.

This policy explains how Junhao Hong, the developer of EatWhat? ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information in the consumer app and on this website. It describes the current local-data design. If the app's data practices change, this policy must be updated to reflect those changes.

The short version: EatWhat? keeps your saved recipes, history and preferences on your device. The consumer app does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

1. Information stored on your device

To provide its features, EatWhat? stores information locally, including:

  • Saved recipe identifiers and browsing or meal-selection history.
  • Meal preferences, filter drafts, excluded foods and dietary restrictions.
  • Allergen restrictions you choose. These choices can be sensitive, so you should consider who can access your device.
  • Language preferences and local application settings.

This information is used to remember your choices and support the app's recommendation and browsing features. It is not sent to a developer-operated server by the current consumer app.

2. Information we do not collect through the app

The current consumer app does not create user accounts, serve advertisements, use analytics SDKs, track you across other companies' apps or websites, or sell your personal information. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or health records to provide its current features.

The current consumer app does not upload your saved recipes, history or restrictions for external AI analysis. Internal development and content-review tools are separate from the consumer app described in this policy.

3. Storage, retention and your controls

Local records remain on your device until you remove them using available app controls or remove the app's local data through your device. Use Settings → My Data to manage the supported local records. Changing a preference does not imply that all history has been deleted.

Device backups and restores are controlled by Apple and your device settings. A backup may contain data that was previously present on the device; deleting a record in the app does not promise removal from every existing backup. We do not operate a cloud-sync or backup service for your EatWhat? records.

4. Support messages

If you choose to contact the developer, the developer receives the information you send, such as your email address, your message, and any screenshots or device details you voluntarily include. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate the reported issue. Please do not send sensitive medical information or personal history that is unnecessary for your request.

Support correspondence may be handled by the email provider used by the developer. The public support channel and its applicable handling arrangements must be confirmed before this policy is published.

5. This website and hosting

This local edition of the website has no advertising, analytics, tracking scripts, account system, contact form, or cookies set by its own code. Its images and styles are included locally; it does not load external fonts or image services.

If this website is deployed to a hosting provider, that provider may process technical request information, such as an IP address, browser information and access logs, to deliver and secure the site. The hosting provider, applicable retention and relevant privacy information must be identified before publication. This local draft does not claim that a future hosting service collects no information.

6. Sharing and external links

We do not sell local app data or share it for targeted advertising. If you independently share a screenshot or send a support message, the service you choose handles that information under its own terms. Any external website you open has its own privacy practices.

7. Security

The app relies on the device's application sandbox and operating-system protections for its local records. Keep your device and operating system up to date, and protect access to the device. No method of storage or communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children

EatWhat? does not operate accounts or request personal information from children through its current consumer features. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of cooking instructions and food-related preferences. The app's final age rating is determined separately through the App Store's age-rating process.

9. Privacy questions and requests

You can manage supported local app records using the app and device controls. Because the developer does not hold a server-side copy of your local profile, the developer cannot retrieve or edit that profile remotely. Where applicable law gives you rights concerning information you have sent to the developer, you may contact the developer to ask about exercising those rights.
